A normal potassium in DKA can fool you 🩸
Most patients arrive with a normal or high potassium, but total body potassium is low. Insulin drives it into cells, so it can fall during treatment: deadly arrhythmias, weak breathing muscles.
The DKA order: fluids, potassium, insulin. In the field: fluids per protocol, and a 12-lead for hyperkalemia changes. Insulin itself waits for the hospital.
