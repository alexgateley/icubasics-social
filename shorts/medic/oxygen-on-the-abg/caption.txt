Normal pH, low oxygen. It happens 🫁
PaO2 is its own question on a blood gas: about 80 to 100 on room air, and below that is hypoxemia, even with a normal pH.
Saturation is normally about 94 to 100%. Note how much oxygen the patient was on when the gas was drawn.
Some COPD patients have a lower target, often 88 to 92%. Oxygen per your protocol.
