Low vs high sodium: why fast fixes hurt the brain 🚑
Low Na: water shifts into brain cells → swelling, confusion, seizures. High Na: cells shrink → thirst, confusion, seizures.
Chronic low sodium gets corrected slowly (common limit about 10 mEq/L in 24 h) to avoid osmotic demyelination; severe symptoms like seizures get 3% hypertonic saline per protocol. High sodium comes down gradually too.
Neuro checks, seizure precautions, strict I&O, and repeat sodium checks on transfers.
