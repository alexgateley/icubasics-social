Sepsis and septic shock are not the same call 🦠
Sepsis: the infection is the trigger, but the body's own response injures the organs.
Septic shock is the sickest subset: a vasopressor needed to keep the MAP at 65 or higher, and lactate still above 2, despite adequate fluids.
Hospital mortality in the Sepsis-3 data: over 40 percent. Fluids, oxygen and pre-alert per your protocol.
