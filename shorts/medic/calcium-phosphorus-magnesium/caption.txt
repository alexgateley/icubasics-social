Calcium, phosphorus and magnesium for medics: three numbers that can stop a heart 🚑
Low calcium: tingling, tetany, carpopedal spasm (Trousseau), long QT, seizures; think of it in dialysis and kidney failure patients.
Torsades: magnesium 2 g IV per protocol.
Magnesium toxicity (think eclampsia drips on transfers): reflexes fade first, then breathing can stop. Stop the magnesium, support the airway, and give calcium gluconate per protocol.
Low magnesium keeps potassium and calcium low.
Watch the monitor, check reflexes, trend the labs on transfers.
