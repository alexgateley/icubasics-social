Why is your transfer patient on heparin AND warfarin? 🚑
Warfarin is slow: protein C falls first, and prothrombin lasts about 60 hours, so the INR can rise before real protection arrives.
Heparin or enoxaparin continues for at least 5 days and until the INR has been 2 or higher for at least 24 hours.
