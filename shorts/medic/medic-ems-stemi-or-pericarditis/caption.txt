ST elevation on the 12-lead. Heart attack, or pericarditis?
Look for the heart attack first: ST depression outside aVR and V1, flat or domed elevation, or lead III taller than II. Pericarditis is smile-shaped elevation in almost every lead with PR dips; not sure, repeat the 12-lead.
