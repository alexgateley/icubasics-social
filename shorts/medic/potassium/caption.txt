High vs low potassium on the monitor 🚑
Hyperkalemia: peaked T waves → widening QRS → sine wave. Calcium stabilizes the membrane but doesn't lower K; insulin + dextrose shifts K into cells, so recheck the glucose.
Hypokalemia: flat T waves, U waves, ST depression. Check magnesium too.
Never IV push potassium.
