ROME: read an ABG in 60 seconds 🚑
Respiratory Opposite, Metabolic Equal.
Respiratory: pH and PaCO2 move opposite. Opioid overdose (not breathing enough) → respiratory acidosis. Anxiety or pain (breathing too fast) → respiratory alkalosis.
Metabolic: pH and HCO3 move together. DKA → metabolic acidosis. Prolonged vomiting → metabolic alkalosis.
Breathing tells the story: slow or shallow breathing holds CO2; fast, deep breathing blows it off.
