On a septic shock transfer, which pressor comes first? 💉
Norepinephrine is first line. If doses keep climbing, guidelines suggest adding vasopressin, then epinephrine if the MAP is still inadequate.
Don't wait for a central line: guidelines suggest starting peripherally rather than delaying. Watch that IV site, because a leak can cause necrosis.
