Crackles, distress, adequate BP: CPAP and nitro 🚑
Model guideline: oxygenation first, CPAP or bilevel before or with the drugs. SL nitroglycerin 0.4 mg if SBP under 160, 0.8 mg at 160 or more, every 5 minutes while SBP stays above 100.
No nitrates within 48 hours of sildenafil, vardenafil or tadalafil. Below 100: no nitro, small fluid bolus, consider norepinephrine. Field furosemide: no immediate benefit for most, and it can harm if it's really pneumonia or sepsis. Your local protocol decides.
