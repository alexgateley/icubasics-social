Sepsis: the first hour starts on your stretcher 🚑
Infection + new confusion, fast breathing, or low BP = think sepsis. Screening tools help, but none is perfect (qSOFA alone is not recommended).
Recognize it early, call ahead to the receiving hospital, and give fluids and oxygen per protocol.
At the hospital: lactate, blood cultures before antibiotics (without delaying them), broad-spectrum antibiotics ideally within 1 h, 30 mL/kg crystalloid for low BP or lactate ≥ 4, norepinephrine if MAP stays < 65.
