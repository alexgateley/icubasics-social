A diver surfaces and gets sick. The bends, or a gas embolism? Watch the clock.
Gas embolism: during the ascent or within a few minutes, and it looks like a stroke. The bends: about half within an hour, 9 in 10 by 6 hours, with joint pain. Both get 100% oxygen and transport toward a chamber.
