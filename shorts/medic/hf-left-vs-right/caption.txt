Crackles or swollen ankles? Where the blood backs up tells you the side 🚑
Left heart failure backs up into the lungs: dyspnea, crackles, orthopnea, waking up breathless, and pulmonary edema with frothy, blood-tinged sputum.
Right heart failure backs up into the body. Left is lungs; right is the rest. Treatment follows your local protocol.
