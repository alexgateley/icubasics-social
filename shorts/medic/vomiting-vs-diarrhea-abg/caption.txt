Vomiting patient or diarrhea patient: which way does the pH go? 🧪
Vomiting loses stomach acid, so alkalosis. Diarrhea loses bicarb, so acidosis.
Fast, deep breathing in a sick patient? The lungs may be blowing off CO2 to compensate for a metabolic acidosis: DKA, shock or sepsis, kidney failure, poisonings like aspirin.
Assess and treat per your protocol.
