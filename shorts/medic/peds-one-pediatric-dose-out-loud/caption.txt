🚑 NREMT practice: pediatric arrest epi, 0.01 or 0.1 mg/kg?
0.01 mg/kg IV/IO = 0.1 mL/kg of 0.1 mg/mL, max 1 mg, every 3–5 min. 0.1 mg/kg is a tenfold error.
