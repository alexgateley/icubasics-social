Altered patient? Check a glucose ⛽
The 15-gram rule is the classroom and hospital version. The national model EMS guideline treats at 60 mg/dL or less: oral glucose for a conscious patient with a patent airway; dextrose IV, or glucagon if there's no IV access, for an unconscious patient or one who can't protect the airway. Use your protocol's numbers and doses.
Reassess vitals and mental status. Check the glucose again if mental status hasn't returned to normal.
Seizure with a low? The guideline says transport.
