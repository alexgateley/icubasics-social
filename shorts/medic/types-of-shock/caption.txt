Types of shock for NREMT: tank, pump, pipes or blockage 🚑
Hypovolemic: fast heart, cool pale skin, flat neck veins. Stop the bleeding first, then volume per protocol.
Cardiogenic: cool clammy skin, crackles, JVD. Go easy on fluids; get a 12-lead early and pick the destination per protocol.
Distributive: often warm skin. Anaphylaxis: IM epinephrine first. Neurogenic after spinal injury: slow heart, warm dry skin. Sepsis: recognize it early and pre-alert the hospital.
Obstructive: tension pneumothorax, tamponade, massive PE; neck veins often bulge. Tension pneumo: needle decompression is a paramedic skill, per protocol.
