DKA vs HHS in the field: storm or drought? 🚑
DKA: hours to a few days, deep rapid breathing, fruity breath, vomiting, belly pain. Often younger, type 1.
HHS: days to weeks, severe dehydration, confusion. Often older, type 2.
Check a glucose. Treat with fluids per protocol, without overdoing it. Get a 12-lead and look for hyperkalemia changes.
Ask why: insulin, ischemia, infection.
Insulin itself waits for the hospital.
