Is this ABG compensated? ⚖️
Find the cause with ROME, then look at the other value.
Uncompensated: the other value is still normal. Anxious, hyperventilating patient: pH 7.53, PaCO2 30, HCO3 24.
Partially compensated: the other value is fighting back, pH still abnormal. Severe diarrhea: pH 7.32, PaCO2 32, HCO3 16.
Fully compensated: both abnormal, pH back in range. Chronic COPD: pH 7.37, PaCO2 56, HCO3 31.
Lungs respond faster; kidneys catch up over days. That's why your COPD patient's "normal" can look very different from everyone else's.
