MAP 65: why that number? 🚑
Estimate MAP from your cuff: DBP + ⅓ pulse pressure, or (SBP + 2 × DBP) ÷ 3. 90/50 ≈ 63.
Below an autoregulation threshold, organ blood flow falls with pressure. In the ICU, Surviving Sepsis sets an initial target of 65 for septic shock on vasopressors; aiming for 80–85 didn't lower deaths.
The number isn't the whole picture: look at mentation, skin and cap refill, urine output and lactate. Follow your protocols for any prehospital pressure target.
