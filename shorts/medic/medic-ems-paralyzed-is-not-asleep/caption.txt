Two paralytics for intubation: succinylcholine and rocuronium. The big difference is the clock.
Sux: on within a minute, gone in 3 to 5. Roc: more than 30 minutes. Neither one touches awareness or pain, so after roc start sedation and pain control right away and keep it going.
