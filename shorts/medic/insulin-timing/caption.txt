Why that insulin patient is low right now 🚑
Ask what they took and when.
Rapid (lispro, aspart): onset about 15 min, peak 1–2 h, lasts 3–5 h.
Short (regular): onset 30–60 min, peak 2–4 h, lasts 6–8 h.
Intermediate (NPH): onset 1–2 h, peak 4–12 h, lasts 12–18 h.
Long (glargine): no pronounced peak, lasts about 24 h.
Insulin still on board can bring the low back, so reassess after treatment and follow your protocol.
