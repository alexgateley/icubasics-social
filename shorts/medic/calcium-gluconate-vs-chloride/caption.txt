Calcium gluconate or calcium chloride: same drug? Not quite 💉
Chloride carries about 3 times the calcium per mL: 27 mg vs 9.3. It's also harsh on veins, and a leak can cause tissue necrosis.
Gluconate is the usual pick for a peripheral IV. Follow your protocol for which one you carry.
On digoxin: avoid calcium, or give it very slowly on the monitor.
