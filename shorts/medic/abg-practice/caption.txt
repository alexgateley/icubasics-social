Your patient has been vomiting for 3 days. pH 7.51, PaCO2 47, HCO3 36.
pH above 7.45 = alkalosis. HCO3 high (base) matches the pH = metabolic alkalosis. PaCO2 a little high = the lungs holding CO2 to compensate, but the pH is still abnormal.
Answer: partially compensated metabolic alkalosis.
Vomiting loses stomach acid, and potassium can drop too: watch the monitor and follow your protocols.
