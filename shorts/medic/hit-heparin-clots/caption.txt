Heparin can cause clots? Know HIT before your next transfer 🚑
In heparin-induced thrombocytopenia, platelets fall, usually 5 to 10 days after starting heparin, and the danger is clotting, not bleeding.
Suspected HIT means all heparin stops and a non-heparin anticoagulant, such as argatroban or bivalirudin, takes over.
