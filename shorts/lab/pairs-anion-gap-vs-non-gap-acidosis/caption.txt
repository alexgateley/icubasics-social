🧪 ASCP MLS/MLT practice: anion gap vs non-gap acidosis
Anion gap = Na − (Cl + HCO3). Normal about 12, but labs vary.
High gap = acid added: ketones, lactate, kidney failure, toxins like methanol and aspirin. Normal gap = bicarb lost: diarrhea, RTA, lots of normal saline.
Low albumin can hide a high gap.
