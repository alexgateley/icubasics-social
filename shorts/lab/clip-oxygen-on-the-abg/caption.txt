🧪 ASCP MLS/MLT practice: PaO2 on the ABG
pH, CO2 and bicarb are acid and base. PaO2 is oxygen, a separate question.
On room air, PaO2 is normally about 80–100. Hypoxemia can sit right next to a normal pH.
Note how much oxygen the patient was on when the gas was drawn.
