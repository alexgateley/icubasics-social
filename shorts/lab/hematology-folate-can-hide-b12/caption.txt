🧪 ASCP MLS/MLT practice: folate can hide B12 deficiency
Low B12 makes large red cells and damages nerves (numbness, tingling, poor balance, a red, sore tongue). Low folate also makes large cells.
B12 needs intrinsic factor from the stomach; in pernicious anemia the immune system destroys it.
Folate alone can fix the blood count while the nerve damage gets worse. Check B12 before giving folate.
