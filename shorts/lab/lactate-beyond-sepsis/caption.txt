🧪 ASCP MLS/MLT practice: lactate beyond sepsis
Normal lactate is under 2 mmol/L. It rises when cells run short of oxygen: shock, sepsis, severe hypoxia.
Also after a seizure, with albuterol or epinephrine, sometimes metformin, and when a failing liver clears it slowly.
Even the draw matters: a long tourniquet or a clenched fist can push it up.
