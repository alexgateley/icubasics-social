🧪 ASCP MLS/MLT practice: tumor lysis syndrome, three up and one down
Potassium, phosphate and uric acid go up; phosphate binds calcium, so calcium goes down.
Uric acid crystals can clog the kidneys. Highest risk: fast-growing blood cancers in the first days after treatment.
Allopurinol stops new uric acid; rasburicase breaks down what's there, after a G6PD check.
