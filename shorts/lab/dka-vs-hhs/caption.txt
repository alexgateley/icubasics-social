🧪 ASCP MLS/MLT practice: DKA vs HHS
DKA: glucose usually 200 or more, pH under 7.30 or bicarb under 18; ketones make the blood acidic.
HHS: glucose 600 or more, effective osmolality over 300, no big ketones.
Insulin pushes potassium into cells, so potassium is checked before insulin.
