🧪 ASCP MLS/MLT practice: MAP 65 in septic shock
MAP = mean arterial pressure, the pressure driving blood into organs. Estimate it: DBP + ⅓ pulse pressure, so 90/50 ≈ 63.
Surviving Sepsis: initial MAP target 65 in septic shock on vasopressors.
MAP isn't the whole picture: lactate and urine output show perfusion too.
