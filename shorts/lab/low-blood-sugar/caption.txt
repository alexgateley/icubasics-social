🧪 ASCP MLS/MLT practice: glucose 62, now what?
Under 70 mg/dL is low.
Rule of 15: 15 grams of fast carbs, wait 15 minutes and recheck. Still under 70? Repeat.
Not alert or can't swallow: glucagon or IV dextrose per protocol.
