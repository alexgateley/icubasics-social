🧪 ASCP MLS/MLT practice: sepsis vs septic shock
Sepsis: life-threatening organ dysfunction from a dysregulated response to infection; at the bedside, a SOFA rise of 2 or more.
Septic shock: a vasopressor needed to keep the MAP at 65 or higher, and lactate still above 2, despite adequate fluids.
