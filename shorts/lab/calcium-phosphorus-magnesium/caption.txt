🧪 ASCP MLS/MLT practice: calcium, phosphorus and magnesium
In kidney failure, phosphorus climbs and calcium falls. Normal total calcium is about 8.8–10.3 mg/dL (ranges vary by lab).
Low albumin makes total calcium read low. Corrected Ca = measured Ca + 0.8 × (4.0 − albumin), but ionized calcium is more reliable.
Low magnesium keeps potassium and calcium low.
