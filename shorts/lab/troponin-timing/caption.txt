🧪 ASCP MLS/MLT practice: troponin timing
Troponin starts to rise about 2–3 h after a heart attack begins and keeps climbing for about a day.
A heart attack needs a rise or fall above the lab's cutoff, plus signs of ischemia. High-sensitivity: low and barely changing over 1–3 h helps rule out.
Up without ischemia = injury: sepsis, PE, kidney disease, even a marathon.
