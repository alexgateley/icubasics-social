🧪 ASCP MLS/MLT practice: potassium before the DKA insulin drip
Most DKA patients arrive with a normal or high potassium, but total body potassium is low.
Insulin drives potassium into cells. Potassium below 3.5? Hold the insulin and replace potassium first (some references use 3.3).
