🧪 ASCP MLS/MLT practice: a creatinine rise that isn't kidney damage
Tubules secrete 10–40% of creatinine's clearance. Trimethoprim and cimetidine block that secretion, so creatinine rises about 0.2–0.4 mg/dL while the GFR doesn't change.
Clue: a small rise soon after starting, with no other sign of injury. Rule out real kidney injury first.
Trimethoprim's potassium rise is real, especially with an ACE inhibitor or weak kidneys.
