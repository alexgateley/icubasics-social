A patient has been vomiting for 3 days. pH 7.51, PaCO2 47, HCO3 36.
1) pH 7.51: above 7.45 = alkalosis.
2) PaCO2 47: slightly high = acid. Doesn't match.
3) HCO3 36: high = base. Matches the pH.
ROME: Metabolic Equal → metabolic alkalosis.
4) Compensation: the lungs hold on to CO2, but the pH is still abnormal.
Answer: partially compensated metabolic alkalosis. Vomiting loses stomach acid; check potassium too.
