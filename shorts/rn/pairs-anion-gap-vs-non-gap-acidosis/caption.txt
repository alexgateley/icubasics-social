Two patients, same low pH, same low bicarb. One has DKA, one has diarrhea. One subtraction tells them apart.
Anion gap = Na − (Cl + HCO3). Gap up: find the added acid, like DKA. Gap normal: find where the bicarb went, like diarrhea.
