Why do heparin and warfarin overlap? ⏳
Protein C, one of the body's own anticoagulants, has a half-life of about 8 hours, so it falls early. Prothrombin (factor II) lasts about 60 hours, so the INR can rise before real protection arrives.
So heparin or enoxaparin continues for at least 5 days AND until the INR has been 2 or higher for at least 24 hours.
