Types of shock for NCLEX: tank, pump, pipes or blockage 🚰
Hypovolemic (tank low): fast heart, cool pale skin, flat neck veins. Stop the bleeding; blood for blood loss, fluids otherwise.
Cardiogenic (pump fails): cool clammy skin, crackles, JVD. Go easy on fluids, support the pump, treat the cause.
Distributive (pipes dilate): the odd one out, often warm skin with high output and low SVR. Septic: antibiotics, fluids, norepinephrine. Anaphylactic: IM epinephrine first. Neurogenic: slow heart, warm dry skin.
Obstructive (blockage): tension pneumothorax, tamponade, massive PE; neck veins often bulge. Relieve the block.
