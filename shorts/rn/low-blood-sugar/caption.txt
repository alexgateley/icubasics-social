Glucose 62. Now what? ⛽
Under 70 mg/dL is low: shaky, sweaty, hungry, pounding heart, confused.
Alert and able to swallow? Rule of 15.
15 grams of fast carbs: 4 oz of juice or regular soda, or glucose tablets.
Wait 15 minutes and recheck. Still under 70? Repeat.
Back above 70 with no meal soon: a carb and protein snack, because insulin may still be working.
Not alert or can't swallow: nothing by mouth. Glucagon or IV dextrose per protocol, and get help.
Beta-blockers can hide the fast heartbeat; sweating may still show.
