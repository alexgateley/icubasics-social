The DKA order: fluids, potassium, insulin.
Most patients arrive with a normal or high potassium, but total body potassium is low. Insulin drives potassium into cells, and about half of patients drop below 3.5 during treatment.
Potassium below 3.5? Hold the insulin and replace potassium first. Some references still use 3.3.
Once it's safe, regular IV insulin per protocol.
