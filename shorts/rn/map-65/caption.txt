Why is the MAP target 65? 🎯
MAP = mean arterial pressure, the pressure driving blood into organs. Estimate it: DBP + ⅓ pulse pressure, or (SBP + 2 × DBP) ÷ 3. So 90/50 ≈ 63.
Organs autoregulate: blood flow stays steady across a pressure range, then falls with pressure below a threshold (for kidneys, maybe near 65).
Surviving Sepsis: initial MAP target 65 in septic shock on vasopressors. Higher targets (80–85) didn't lower deaths and came with more atrial fibrillation.
MAP isn't the whole picture: check lactate, urine output, mentation and cap refill.
