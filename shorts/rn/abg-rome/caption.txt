ROME: the ABG trick in 60 seconds 🏛️
R.O.M.E. = Respiratory Opposite, Metabolic Equal.
Step 1: pH. Below 7.35 = acidosis, above 7.45 = alkalosis.
Respiratory opposite: pH ↓ + PaCO2 ↑ = respiratory acidosis (opioid overdose, COPD). pH ↑ + PaCO2 ↓ = respiratory alkalosis (anxiety, pain).
Metabolic equal: pH ↓ + HCO3 ↓ = metabolic acidosis (DKA, diarrhea). pH ↑ + HCO3 ↑ = metabolic alkalosis (vomiting, NG suction).
Normal PaCO2 about 35–45, HCO3 about 22–26. Ranges vary by lab.
