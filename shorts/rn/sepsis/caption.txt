Sepsis: why the first hour matters ⏱️
Bacteria invade, the body's defenses overreact, vessels widen and leak, BP drops, and organs get less oxygen.
Think sepsis: infection + new confusion, fast breathing, or low blood pressure. Screening tools help, but don't rely on qSOFA alone.
First hour: measure lactate → blood cultures before antibiotics (don't delay them) → broad-spectrum antibiotics, ideally within 1 h when shock or sepsis is likely → 30 mL/kg crystalloid for low BP or lactate ≥ 4 (given within 3 h) → norepinephrine first if MAP stays < 65.
