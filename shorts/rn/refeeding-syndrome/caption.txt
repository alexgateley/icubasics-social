Why can feeding a malnourished patient be dangerous? 🍽️
Refeeding syndrome: insulin drives phosphate, potassium and magnesium into cells, and blood levels crash, usually within 2 to 5 days.
Low phosphate is the hallmark. Heart failure, arrhythmias and respiratory failure can follow.
Give thiamine before feeding, start low and go slow, and check and replace electrolytes daily at first.
