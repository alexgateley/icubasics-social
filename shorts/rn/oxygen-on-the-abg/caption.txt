A normal pH does not mean the oxygen is fine 🫁
pH, CO2 and bicarb are acid and base. PaO2 is oxygen, a separate question.
On room air, PaO2 is normally about 80 to 100. Below that is hypoxemia, and it can sit right next to a normal pH.
Check the saturation too, normally about 94 to 100%, and note how much oxygen the patient was on when the gas was drawn.
Some COPD patients have a lower target, often 88 to 92%. Follow the order and your protocol.
