Is this ABG compensated? 3 answers ⚖️
First find the cause with ROME (Respiratory Opposite, Metabolic Equal). Then look at the other value and the pH.
Uncompensated: the other value is still normal. Anxiety: pH 7.53, PaCO2 30, HCO3 24 = uncompensated respiratory alkalosis.
Partially compensated: the other value is fighting back, pH still abnormal. Severe diarrhea: pH 7.32, PaCO2 32, HCO3 16 = partially compensated metabolic acidosis.
Fully compensated: both abnormal, pH back in 7.35–7.45. Use 7.40 as the midpoint. Chronic COPD: pH 7.37, PaCO2 56, HCO3 31 = fully compensated respiratory acidosis.
Lungs respond faster; kidneys catch up over days. Ranges vary by lab.
