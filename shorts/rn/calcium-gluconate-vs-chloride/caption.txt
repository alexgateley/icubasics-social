Calcium gluconate or calcium chloride? 💉
Chloride carries about 3 times the calcium: 27 mg per mL vs 9.3. But it's harsh on veins, and a leak can cause tissue necrosis.
So gluconate is the usual pick for a peripheral IV.
Two cautions: with digoxin, avoid calcium or give it very slowly with ECG monitoring. And never run it with ceftriaxone in the same Y-site.
