Vomiting or diarrhea: which one is acidosis? 🧪
Vomiting loses stomach acid, so alkalosis. Diarrhea loses bicarb, so acidosis.
Metabolic acidosis: DKA, lactic acidosis from shock or sepsis, kidney failure, severe diarrhea, poisonings like aspirin.
Metabolic alkalosis: vomiting, NG suction, diuretics, low potassium, too many antacids.
