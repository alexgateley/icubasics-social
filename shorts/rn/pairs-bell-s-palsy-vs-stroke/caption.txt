Half the face is drooping. Stroke, or Bell's palsy? Look at the forehead.
Ask them to raise both eyebrows. Whole side weak, forehead included: think Bell's palsy. A droop that spares the forehead, or any arm weakness or speech trouble: stroke until proven otherwise. Get the last known well time.
