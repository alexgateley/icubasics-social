No. Glucose falls long before the acid clears.
Below 250: add dextrose to the fluids and keep the insulin running.
Check glucose every 1 to 2 hours, or per protocol, and electrolytes, venous pH and ketones about every 4.
Many protocols and exams watch the anion gap close. The 2024 consensus goes by ketones plus pH or bicarb.
