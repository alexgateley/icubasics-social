Sepsis or septic shock: do you know the line between them? 🦠
Sepsis: life-threatening organ dysfunction caused by a dysregulated host response to infection. At the bedside, an acute rise of 2 or more points on the SOFA score.
Septic shock is the sickest subset: a vasopressor needed to keep the MAP at 65 or higher, and lactate still above 2, despite adequate fluids.
Hospital mortality in septic shock was over 40 percent in the Sepsis-3 data.
