Septic shock and no central line yet. Do you wait? 💉
No. Guidelines suggest starting vasopressors peripherally rather than delaying.
Norepinephrine is first line. If doses keep climbing, guidelines suggest adding vasopressin, usually around 0.25 to 0.5 mcg/kg/min of norepinephrine. MAP still inadequate? Epinephrine can be added next.
Watch the IV site closely: norepinephrine leaking into tissue can cause necrosis, and the label calls for phentolamine.
