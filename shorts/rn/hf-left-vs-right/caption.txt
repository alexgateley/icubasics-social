Left or right heart failure? Ask where the blood backs up 🫁
Left fails: blood backs up into the lungs. Dyspnea, crackles that don't clear with a cough, orthopnea, waking up breathless at night (PND), and at its worst pulmonary edema with frothy, blood-tinged sputum.
Right fails: blood backs up into the body.
Left is lungs. Right is the rest. Save this for NCLEX.
