The heparin complication that causes clots, not bleeding 🩸
HIT, heparin-induced thrombocytopenia, is an immune reaction: platelets fall, usually 5 to 10 days after starting heparin, or sooner if the patient had heparin in the last month.
Platelets are checked before and during heparin. Suspected HIT: stop all heparin and start a non-heparin anticoagulant, such as argatroban or bivalirudin.
