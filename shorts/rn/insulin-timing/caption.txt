Insulin onset, peak and duration in 60 seconds 🏃
Rapid (lispro, aspart): onset about 15 min, peak 1–2 h, lasts 3–5 h. Have the meal ready.
Short (regular): onset 30–60 min, peak 2–4 h, lasts 6–8 h. The classic IV insulin.
Intermediate (NPH): onset 1–2 h, peak 4–12 h, lasts 12–18 h. Morning NPH can mean an afternoon low.
Long (glargine): onset about 1.5–4 h, no pronounced peak, lasts about 24 h. It can still cause a low.
A low is most likely near the peak. Times are approximate and sources differ; follow your facility's references.
