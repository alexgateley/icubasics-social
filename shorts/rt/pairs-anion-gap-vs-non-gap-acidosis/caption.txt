🫁 NBRC practice: anion gap vs non-gap acidosis
Same low pH, same low bicarb. One subtraction tells them apart.
Anion gap = sodium − (chloride + bicarb), normal about 12 (labs vary). High gap: acid was added. Normal gap: bicarb was lost. Low albumin can hide a high gap.
