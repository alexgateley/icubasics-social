🫁 NBRC practice: ready for the breathing trial
Ready to come off the vent? Screen every day.
Trial: low pressure support, CPAP or T-piece, 30 minutes to 2 hours. Stop for RR over 35, SpO₂ under 90%, or 2+ distress signs. Extubation also needs alertness, a good cough and manageable secretions.
