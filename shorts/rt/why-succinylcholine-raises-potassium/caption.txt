🫁 NBRC practice: why succinylcholine raises potassium
Succinylcholine is the paralytic that raises potassium.
After burns, major trauma or denervation, potassium can surge into arrest. It can also trigger malignant hyperthermia: jaw spasm, rising CO₂, tachycardia, heat.
