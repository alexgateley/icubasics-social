🫁 NBRC practice: nasal cannula math
Every liter on a nasal cannula adds about 4% oxygen.
FiO₂ ≈ 20 + 4 × L/min: 1 L ≈ 24%, 6 L ≈ 44%. Past 6 L/min the cannula is maxed out, so step up the device.
