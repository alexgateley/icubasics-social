🫁 NBRC practice: predicted or actual body weight
In ARDS, breath size comes from height, not the scale.
6 mL/kg of predicted body weight. Men 50 kg, women 45.5 kg, + 2.3 kg per inch over 5 ft. A 5 ft 10 in man: 73 kg, about 440 mL. Then plateau 30 or less.
