🫁 NBRC practice: how long will the tank last
You're moving a patient on a portable tank. Will the oxygen last the trip?
Minutes = gauge pressure × tank factor ÷ liter flow. E tank factor 0.28. Plan a reserve: about 30 extra minutes.
