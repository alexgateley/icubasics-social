🫁 NBRC practice: reading the capnography box
Every breath out draws a box on the monitor. Learn to read it.
EtCO₂ normal is 35–45. Climbing: usually breathing too slow or too shallow. Falling: fast breathing, or less blood flow to the lungs. On the vent, use end tidal to adjust the rate.
