🫁 NBRC practice: breathe in or blow out
Two plastic devices at the bedside, and they're easy to mix up.
Incentive spirometer = breathe IN slowly, hold 3 to 5 s, 10 to 15 breaths every 1 to 2 hours. Oscillating PEP = blow OUT to loosen mucus, then huff.
