🫁 NBRC practice: flat line or slow slide
End tidal just fell. Did the tube come out, or is the heart failing?
Sudden flat line: tube out or disconnected until proven otherwise; check the tube and the circuit. Slow slide over minutes: less CO₂ reaching the lungs, think shock.
