🫁 NBRC practice: AC, SIMV or pressure support
Assist control, SIMV, pressure support: who starts each breath, and who ends it?
AC: every breath full. SIMV: set breaths full, extras the patient's own size. Pressure support: patient starts every breath, ends when flow tapers; no set rate, so it needs a working drive.
