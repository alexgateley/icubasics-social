🫁 NBRC practice: RSBI under 105
Ready to breathe without the ventilator? One number helps: the rapid shallow breathing index.
RSBI = breaths per minute ÷ tidal volume in liters. Under 105 suggests success. Units trap: 400 mL = 0.4 L. AARC 2024: not needed before a breathing trial.
