🫁 NBRC practice: ARDS or cardiogenic edema
Both lungs are white on the x-ray. Is it the heart, or the lung itself?
Cardiogenic edema is a pressure problem: diuretics, often nitrates. ARDS is a leak: small breaths, 6 mL/kg of ideal body weight, sized by height, not the scale.
