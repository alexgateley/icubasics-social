🫁 NBRC practice: high peak, tube or lungs
The peak pressure is climbing. Is it the tube or the lungs? Check the plateau.
High peak, normal plateau: resistance (secretions, kinked or bitten tube, bronchospasm). Both high: stiff lungs or chest. Keep the plateau at 30 or less.
