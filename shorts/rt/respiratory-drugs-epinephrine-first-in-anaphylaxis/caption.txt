🫁 NBRC practice: wheezing in anaphylaxis
Wheezing and hives after a bee sting. Albuterol first? No.
Epinephrine IM into the mid outer thigh goes first. Still wheezing? Add albuterol; it doesn't replace epinephrine. Watch for a second reaction hours later.
