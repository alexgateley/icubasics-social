🫁 NBRC practice: the bends or gas embolism
A diver surfaces and gets sick. The bends, or a gas embolism?
Gas embolism is fast and looks like a stroke (often breath-holding on the way up). The bends is slower: joint pain, rash, numbness. Both get 100% oxygen and transport toward a chamber.
