🫁 NBRC practice: paralyzed is not asleep
Two paralytics for intubation. The big difference is the clock.
Succinylcholine: gone in 3 to 5 minutes. Rocuronium: 30+ minutes, long after the induction drug wears off. Start sedation and pain control right away for the ventilated patient.
