🫁 NBRC practice: pulse ox stuck at 85
The pulse ox reads 85. You turn the oxygen up. It still reads 85.
SpO2 stuck near 85% with a high PaO2 = think methemoglobin. Ask for co-oximetry. Antidote: methylene blue.
