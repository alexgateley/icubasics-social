🫁 NBRC practice: vent alarms, high vs low
Ventilator alarming? Look at the patient before the screen.
High pressure = resistance; low pressure = a leak. Peak climbs but the plateau doesn't? Resistance, like secretions or a kink. Crashing: DOPE, and bag by hand.
