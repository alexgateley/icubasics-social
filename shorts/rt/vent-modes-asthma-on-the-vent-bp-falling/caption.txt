🫁 NBRC practice: asthma on the vent, BP falling
Asthma on the vent, and the blood pressure is falling? Think trapped air.
Auto-PEEP: expiratory flow never gets back to zero. Give time to breathe out: lower rate, shorter inspiration, smaller tidal volume. Turning up the rate makes it worse.
