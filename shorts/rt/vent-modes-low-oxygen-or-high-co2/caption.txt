🫁 NBRC practice: low oxygen or high CO₂
Low oxygen or high carbon dioxide on the vent? Different dials.
CO₂ follows minute ventilation: raise the rate or tidal volume. O₂ follows FiO₂ and PEEP. More oxygen won't fix the CO₂.
