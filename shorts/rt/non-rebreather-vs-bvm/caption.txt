🫁 NBRC practice: non-rebreather or BVM
SpO₂ 100% on a non-rebreather, and the patient is still in trouble. How?
Slow, shallow breaths of nearly pure oxygen can hold the saturation while CO₂ climbs. SpO₂ shows oxygenation, EtCO₂ shows ventilation: check both.
