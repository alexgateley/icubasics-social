💊 PTCE practice: which diabetes drugs cause lows
Sulfonylureas: glipizide, glyburide, glimepiride. Meglitinides: repaglinide.
They push insulin out of the pancreas whatever the glucose is doing. Metformin alone rarely causes a low.
Sulfonylurea lows can last a day or two, or longer.
