💊 PTCE practice: ACE inhibitors (-pril) vs ARBs (-sartan)
Prils jam the ACE enzyme; sartans block the angiotensin II receptor.
ACE also clears bradykinin, so prils can cause a dry cough and, rarely, angioedema.
Both can raise potassium, and both are off limits in pregnancy.
