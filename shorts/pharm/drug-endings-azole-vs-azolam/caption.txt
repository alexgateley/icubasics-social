💊 PTCE practice: -azole vs -azolam
Fluconazole is an azole antifungal. Midazolam and alprazolam are benzodiazepines.
Fluconazole slows midazolam's breakdown, so levels rise and sedation can deepen.
Not every -azole is an antifungal: metronidazole is an antibiotic, and omeprazole lowers stomach acid.
