💊 PTCE practice: -olol, -pril, -sartan, -dipine
-olol: beta blocker (metoprolol). -pril: ACE inhibitor (lisinopril). -sartan: ARB (losartan). -dipine: calcium channel blocker (amlodipine).
Exceptions: carvedilol and labetalol block alpha and beta; diltiazem and verapamil are calcium channel blockers with no -dipine.
The ending is a clue, not a guarantee.
