💊 PTCE practice: diltiazem vs amlodipine
Same class, calcium channel blockers, different target.
Amlodipine mostly hits vessel muscle and lowers pressure. Ankle swelling is common and dose related.
Diltiazem also reaches the AV node: rate control in AFib and flutter, but out in reduced-EF heart failure.
