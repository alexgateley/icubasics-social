💊 PTCE practice: four insulin types
Rapid (lispro) peaks in about 1–2 h. Regular: 2–4 h. NPH (cloudy): 4–12 h. Glargine: no pronounced peak.
Mixing: clear before cloudy. Glargine never mixes.
NPH and glargine never go IV.
