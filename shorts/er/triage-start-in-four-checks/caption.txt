🚑 CEN practice: RR 34 at a mass casualty, red or yellow?
START: Walk? Breathing once the airway is opened? Over 30? Radial pulse or refill over 2 seconds? Follows a simple command? Remember 30, 2, can do.
Open airways and stop major bleeding, then keep moving.
