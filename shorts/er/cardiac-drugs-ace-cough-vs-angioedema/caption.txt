🚑 CEN practice: ACE inhibitor cough vs angioedema
Dry cough: report it; the usual switch is an ARB. Angioedema: lips, tongue, face or airway swelling, without hives, even years in.
Airway first: epinephrine, steroids and antihistamines haven't been shown to work for this kind. No ACE inhibitor again.
