🚑 CEN practice: belly pain with labs and a CT, which ED triage level?
Lifesaving intervention now: level 1. High risk: level 2. Everyone else, count resource types: two or more = 3, one = 4, none = 5.
Oral meds, a splint and the exam don't count. Clearly abnormal vital signs on a recheck can move a patient up to level 2.
