🚑 CEN practice: calcium, phosphorus and magnesium
Low calcium: tingling, tetany, Chvostek and Trousseau signs, a long QT, seizures.
Low magnesium keeps potassium and calcium low. IV magnesium treats torsades.
Too much magnesium: reflexes fade first, then breathing can stop. IV calcium gluconate counters it (per protocol).
