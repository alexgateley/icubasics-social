🚑 CEN practice: DKA vs HHS
DKA: hours to a few days, ketones make the blood acidic. Glucose usually 200 or more, pH under 7.30 or bicarb under 18.
HHS: days to weeks. Glucose 600 or more, effective osmolality over 300, no big ketones, often confused.
Both: fluids first. Check potassium before insulin, because insulin pushes potassium into cells.
