🚑 CEN practice: calcium channel blockers
Dipines (amlodipine, nifedipine) dilate arteries and don't slow the AV node. Diltiazem and verapamil slow rate and AV conduction.
Not with a systolic under 90 or in reduced-EF heart failure. Add a beta blocker and watch for heart block.
