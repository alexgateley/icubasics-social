🚑 CEN practice: digoxin Fab and potassium
Adult K⁺ above 5.5 with worsening toxicity is a reason for digoxin immune Fab. After Fab, potassium can drop fast: check it often.
Draw the digoxin level before Fab if you can; afterward a standard level misleads for days.
