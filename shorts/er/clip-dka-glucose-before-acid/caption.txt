🚑 CEN practice: DKA: glucose falls before the acid clears
Glucose falls long before the acid clears. Below 250: add dextrose and keep the insulin running.
Check glucose every 1 to 2 hours, and electrolytes, venous pH and ketones about every 4.
The 2024 consensus goes by ketones plus pH or bicarb.
