🚑 CEN practice: the acetylcysteine clock
Acetaminophen overdose can look fine for a day. Protection is greatest when acetylcysteine starts within 8 hours.
Level at 4 h or later; if it won't be back by 8 h, start the load. Arrived late? Still give it.
