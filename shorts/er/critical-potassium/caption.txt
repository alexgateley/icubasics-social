🚑 CEN practice: critical potassium
Normal 3.5–5.0. 6–6.5 needs prompt attention; above 6.5, more aggressive treatment. The ECG doesn't track the number closely, so get one.
A hard draw can falsely raise it, but ECG changes don't wait for a redraw.
