🚑 CEN practice: adult not breathing after the airway is opened, black or red?
In a mass casualty: black. No rescue breaths and no CPR during triage; the goal flips to the most good for the most people.
Exceptions: JumpSTART gives a child with a pulse five rescue breaths, and after a lightning strike the ones who look dead go first.
