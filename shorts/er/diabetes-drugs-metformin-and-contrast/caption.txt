🚑 CEN practice: metformin and contrast
Boxed warning: lactic acidosis. Below an eGFR of 30 it's contraindicated; stop it at or before the study at eGFR 30–60.
Recheck the eGFR 48 hours later; restart only if kidney function is stable.
