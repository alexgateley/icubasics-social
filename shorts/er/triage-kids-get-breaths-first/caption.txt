🚑 CEN practice: a child at an MCI isn't breathing, black tag or rescue breaths?
Children usually arrest from a breathing problem. JumpSTART: open the airway; still not breathing with a pulse, give five rescue breaths. Breathes = red, still not = black.
SALT gives the same child two rescue breaths. Local protocol decides.
