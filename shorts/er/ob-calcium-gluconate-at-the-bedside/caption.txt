🚑 CEN practice: calcium gluconate at the bedside
On magnesium sulfate, an injectable calcium salt should be immediately available: calcium gluconate 1 g IV (10 mL of 10%), given slowly.
It doesn't remove the magnesium: stopping the infusion is still the first action.
