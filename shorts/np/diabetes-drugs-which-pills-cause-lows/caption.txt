🩺 FNP boards practice: which diabetes drugs cause hypoglycemia
Metformin alone rarely does. Sulfonylureas (glipizide, glyburide, glimepiride) and meglitinides (repaglinide) push insulin out whatever the glucose is doing.
Highest risk: older patients, kidney or liver disease, not eating, alcohol, and adding other diabetes drugs to insulin or a sulfonylurea.
Sulfonylurea lows can last a day or two, or longer.
