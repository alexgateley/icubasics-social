🩺 FNP boards practice: SIADH vs DI
Urine specific gravity splits them: DI usually under 1.005 with huge dilute volumes; SIADH concentrated, little urine, low sodium.
DI gets water back, plus desmopressin if central. SIADH gets fluids restricted.
Trap: cerebral salt wasting looks like SIADH on labs but needs saline, not fluid restriction.
