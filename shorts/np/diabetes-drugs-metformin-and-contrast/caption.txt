🩺 FNP boards practice: metformin, contrast and eGFR
Boxed warning: lactic acidosis. Contraindicated below an eGFR of 30.
Label: stop it at or before a contrast study if the eGFR is 30–60, or with liver disease, alcoholism, heart failure, or intra-arterial contrast.
Recheck the eGFR 48 hours later, and restart only if kidney function is stable.
