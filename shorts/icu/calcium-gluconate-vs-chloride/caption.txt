Gluconate or chloride: do you know why it matters? 💉
27 mg of calcium per mL in chloride, 9.3 in gluconate. Chloride is about 3 times stronger and harsh on veins: slowly, preferably central or deep, and a leak can cause necrosis.
Gluconate is the usual pick for a peripheral IV.
On digoxin: avoid calcium, or give it very slowly on ECG monitoring. Never with ceftriaxone in the same Y-site.
