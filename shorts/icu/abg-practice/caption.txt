Prolonged vomiting: pH 7.51, PaCO2 47, HCO3 36.
pH up + HCO3 up = metabolic alkalosis (Metabolic Equal). PaCO2 up = respiratory compensation, pH still out of range → partially compensated metabolic alkalosis.
ICU check: expected PaCO2 in metabolic alkalosis ≈ 0.7 × HCO3 + 21 (± 2) ≈ 46 (44 to 48), so 47 is appropriate compensation, not a second respiratory problem.
Vomiting = chloride and acid loss; potassium often falls too. Check K+.
