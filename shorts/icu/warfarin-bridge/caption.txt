INR just reached 2. Stop the heparin? Not so fast ⏳
Factor VII lasts only hours, so the INR moves early, while prothrombin (factor II) lasts about 60 hours. Protein C falls first, at about 8 hours.
The rule: parenteral anticoagulation for at least 5 days and until the INR has been 2 or higher for at least 24 hours.
