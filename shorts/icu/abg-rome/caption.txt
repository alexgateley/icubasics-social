ROME for ABGs, the 60-second version 🏛️
Respiratory Opposite: pH and PaCO2 move in opposite directions (hypoventilation from opioids or COPD → respiratory acidosis; anxiety, pain, sepsis → respiratory alkalosis).
Metabolic Equal: pH and HCO3 move together (DKA, lactic acidosis, diarrhea → metabolic acidosis; vomiting, NG suction, diuretics → metabolic alkalosis).
Whichever value matches the pH is the primary process. At the bedside, also check whether the other value compensates as expected (Winter's formula for metabolic acidosis) to catch a mixed disorder.
Ranges vary by lab (teaching set: pH 7.35–7.45, PaCO2 35–45, HCO3 22–26).
