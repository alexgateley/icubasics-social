Compensated or not? ⚖️
Uncompensated: the secondary value is still normal (anxiety: 7.53 / 30 / 24).
Partially compensated: the secondary value moved, pH still out of range (diarrhea: 7.32 / 32 / 16; Winter's predicts 1.5 × 16 + 8 = 32 ± 2, so this is appropriate compensation).
Fully compensated: pH back in range, both values abnormal (chronic COPD: 7.37 / 56 / 31).
ICU reality: complete compensation is uncommon; most responses bring the pH only partway back. A normal pH with two very abnormal values should make you think mixed disorder.
