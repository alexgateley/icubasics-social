MAP 65: where the number comes from 📈
SSC 2021: initial MAP target 65 mmHg for septic shock on vasopressors (strong recommendation, moderate-quality evidence). It is an initial target, not a floor; individualize.
SEPSISPAM (776 patients): 80–85 vs 65–70 gave no difference in 28- or 90-day mortality, more new atrial fibrillation; the chronic hypertension subgroup needed less RRT with the higher target.
65 trial (patients 65+, vasodilatory hypotension): permissive 60–65 had similar 90-day mortality (41.0% vs 43.8%) with less vasopressor exposure.
Renal autoregulation may fail closer to 65 than 80. Diastolic pressure is the upstream pressure for LV perfusion. MAP alone isn't perfusion: follow lactate, urine output, mentation and capillary refill.
