How do vasopressors actually raise blood pressure? 🩺
MAP ≈ cardiac output × SVR. Norepinephrine squeezes the vessels (alpha-1) and gives the heart a modest push (beta-1); vasopressin adds a separate squeeze through V1 receptors. First-line in septic shock per Surviving Sepsis 2021, initial MAP target 65.
