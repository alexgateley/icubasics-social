Sepsis: the first hour, by the Surviving Sepsis Campaign 🩸
Lactate (suggested). Blood cultures as soon as possible, ideally before antimicrobials, without delaying them. Antimicrobials ideally within 1 h for septic shock or probable sepsis (possible sepsis without shock: rapid workup, within 3 h if concern persists).
30 mL/kg crystalloid in the first 3 h for hypoperfusion: still a weak/conditional, low-certainty suggestion in SSC 2026; use adjusted or ideal body weight if BMI > 30. Balanced crystalloids preferred.
Norepinephrine first, initial MAP target 65. qSOFA is not a stand-alone screen: NEWS, MEWS or SIRS preferred.
