Sodium: the fix can hurt more than the problem 🧠
Hyponatremia: water moves into brain cells → cerebral edema. Hypernatremia: cells shrink.
Chronic hyponatremia: limit the rise (commonly about 10 mEq/L in 24 h; 8 or less if high ODS risk) to avoid osmotic demyelination. Severe symptoms like seizures: 3% hypertonic saline per protocol. Hypernatremia: lower it gradually to avoid cerebral edema.
Neuro checks, seizure precautions, strict I&O, sodium every few hours, and watch for a sudden rise in urine output.
