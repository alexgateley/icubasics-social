Platelets falling on day 7 of a heparin drip? Think HIT 🧪
It's an immune reaction, and the danger is clotting, not bleeding. Suspected HIT: stop all heparin and start a non-heparin anticoagulant, such as argatroban or bivalirudin.
And confirm every heparin vial and its strength: mix-ups have caused fatal bleeding.
