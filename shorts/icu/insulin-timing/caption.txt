Most insulins have a danger window 🏃
Rapid (lispro, aspart): onset about 15 min, peak 1–2 h, lasts 3–5 h.
Short (regular): onset 30–60 min, peak 2–4 h, lasts 6–8 h SC. It's the classic drip insulin; IV it starts in about 10–15 min and ends in about 4 h (Humulin R label).
Intermediate (NPH): onset 1–2 h, peak 4–12 h, lasts 12–18 h. Never IV.
Long (glargine): onset about 1.5–4 h, no pronounced peak, about 24 h. Never IV, never mixed.
Tube feeds interrupted? Insulin is still working, so a low can follow. IV dextrose may be given while it's active, per your protocol.
Times are approximate; follow your facility's references.
