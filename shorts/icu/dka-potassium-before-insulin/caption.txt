Potassium 3.1 in DKA and the insulin order is in. Don't hang it yet 🩸
Total body potassium is low even when the first number looks normal or high. Hypokalemia below 3.5 hit about half of patients in treatment.
Below 3.5: hold insulin, replace potassium first. Some references still use 3.3.
Then regular IV insulin per protocol, and keep replacing potassium as ordered.
