MAP 65 on norepinephrine, lactate 3.6, fluids already in: that's septic shock 🦠
Sepsis-3: organ dysfunction from a dysregulated host response to infection, an acute SOFA rise of 2 or more.
Septic shock: vasopressor needed for MAP 65 or higher, plus lactate above 2, despite adequate fluids.
Hospital mortality in the Sepsis-3 data: over 40 percent.
