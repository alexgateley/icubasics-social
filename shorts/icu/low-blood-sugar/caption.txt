Glucose 62 on the unit. Now what? ⛽
Under 70 mg/dL is low. Under 54 is more serious.
Alert and swallowing: 15 g of fast carbs, recheck in 15 minutes, repeat if still under 70.
Not alert, or can't swallow: nothing by mouth. IV dextrose or glucagon per protocol, and get help.
On an insulin drip, follow your drip protocol for the hold and the recheck. Lows are common in treated DKA, about 16–28%.
Beta-blockers can hide the fast heartbeat; sweating may still show.
