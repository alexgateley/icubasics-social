MAP inadequate and the central line isn't in. Start the pressor anyway 💉
Norepinephrine first. Vasopressin usually added around 0.25 to 0.5 mcg/kg/min of norepinephrine, epinephrine next if the MAP is still inadequate.
Peripheral start per SSC 2021: short term, in a vein in or above the antecubital fossa.
Extravasation can cause necrosis. The label calls for phentolamine, as soon as possible.
