NG suction running and the bicarb is climbing. Why? 🧪
Acid lost: metabolic alkalosis. Vomiting, NG suction, diuretics, low potassium, too many antacids.
Metabolic acidosis: DKA, lactic acidosis, kidney failure, severe diarrhea, poisonings like aspirin. The lungs compensate with rapid, deep breathing.
Vomiting loses stomach acid. Diarrhea loses bicarb.
