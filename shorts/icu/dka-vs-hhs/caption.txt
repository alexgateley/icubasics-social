DKA vs HHS: the storm and the drought ⛈️
DKA: hours to a few days. Ketones and acidosis lead. Glucose usually 200 or more or known diabetes, ketones up, pH under 7.30 or bicarb under 18. Euglycemic DKA (glucose under 200) is about 10% of DKA, so stop SGLT2 inhibitors on admission.
HHS: days to weeks. Dehydration leads. Glucose 600 or more, effective osmolality over 300, pH at least 7.30, bicarb at least 15.
More than a third of crises overlap, so check both ketones and osmolality.
Both: fluids first, potassium before insulin (hold insulin under 3.5; some references still use 3.3).
