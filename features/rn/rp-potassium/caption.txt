Hyperkalemia vs hypokalemia: what each does to the heart ⚡
High K: peaked T waves → wide QRS → sine wave. Calcium gluconate stabilizes the membrane (it doesn't lower K); insulin + dextrose shifts K into cells, so watch the glucose.
Low K: flat T waves, U waves, ST depression. Check magnesium, or the potassium won't correct.
Never IV push potassium. Dilute it, use a pump, stay within your policy's max rate.
