Balanced or unbalanced fluids: what's the difference? 💧
Plasma chloride runs about 98 to 106. Normal saline carries 154 with no buffer. Lactated Ringer's carries 109 plus lactate, which the body turns into bicarbonate.
Liters of saline can cause a hyperchloremic metabolic acidosis with a normal anion gap.
