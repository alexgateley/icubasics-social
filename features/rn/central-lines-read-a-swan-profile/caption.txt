📊 Read a Swan profile in three steps
Pump (CI 2.5–4.0), tanks (CVP 2–6 right, wedge 8–12 left), squeeze (SVR 800–1200). Hypovolemic, cardiogenic and obstructive all squeeze high; distributive is the low-SVR odd one out.
