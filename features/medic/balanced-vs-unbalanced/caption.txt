Why does the fluid you hang matter on a long transport? 💧
Normal saline carries 154 chloride and no buffer. LR carries 109 plus lactate, which the body turns into bicarbonate.
Large volumes of saline can cause a hyperchloremic metabolic acidosis with a normal anion gap. Fluid choice follows your local protocol.
