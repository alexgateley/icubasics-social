🫁 NBRC practice: the Apgar never decides
The Apgar score never tells you when to start resuscitation.
A baby who isn't breathing gets breaths within the first minute, before the first score exists. Scored at 1 and 5 minutes; 7 to 10 at five minutes is considered normal.
