🫁 NBRC practice: is the tube in? Check the wave
Breath sounds, chest rise and fog in the tube can all mislead.
Waveform capnography is the most reliable check: a real wave with every breath out, steady or growing, over seven breaths. No trace = wrong place. Keep it on, and recheck after every move.
