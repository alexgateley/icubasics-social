🫁 NBRC practice: EtCO₂ under 25
No tube, and the EtCO₂ reads 22. What is that telling you?
Normal is 35 to 45. Poor perfusion, or fast breathing to blow off acid, drops it. Under 25 may be a sign of poor perfusion: look for shock.
