🫁 NBRC practice: CPAP or BiPAP
CPAP or bilevel? One holds things open. The other also helps you breathe.
CPAP: one constant pressure, splints open, helps oxygenation. BiPAP: EPAP + IPAP; the gap is pressure support and moves CO2.
