🫁 NBRC practice: MDI or DPI
Two inhalers, two different breaths. Mix them up, and less drug reaches the lungs.
MDI: shake, breathe out, press as you start a SLOW deep breath. DPI: no spray; seal lips, breathe in deeply (some need it forceful), never exhale into it.
