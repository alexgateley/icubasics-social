🫁 NBRC practice: albuterol vs ipratropium
Albuterol and ipratropium both open the airways. They are not the same drug.
Albuterol: beta-2 agonist, starts in about 5 minutes, the rescue drug. Ipratropium: anticholinergic, 15–30 minutes to start, added to albuterol, never instead.
