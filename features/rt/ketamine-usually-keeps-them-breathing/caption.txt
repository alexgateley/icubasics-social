🫁 NBRC practice: ketamine and breathing
Ketamine sedates deeply and usually keeps them breathing.
The drive to breathe usually stays, but push it fast or give too much and apnea can happen; laryngospasm is uncommon but real. Monitor continuously, airway equipment ready.
By Alex G, BSN, RN, CEN, CCRN, CMC, NREMT-P. Education only; follow your institution's protocols.
