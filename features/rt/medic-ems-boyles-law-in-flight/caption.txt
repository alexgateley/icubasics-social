🫁 NBRC practice: Boyle's law in flight
Climb in an aircraft, and every pocket of trapped gas gets bigger.
Pressure down, volume up: at an 8,000 ft cabin, trapped gas grows by about a third. Check the ET cuff pressure with a manometer on the climb and the descent.
