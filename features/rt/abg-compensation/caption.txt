🫁 NBRC practice: is this ABG compensated?
Is this ABG compensated? Find the cause with ROME, then check the other value and the pH.
Uncompensated: the other value is normal. Partial: it's fighting back, pH still abnormal. Full: both abnormal, pH back in range. Lungs respond fast; kidneys take days.
