Vomiting for three days. pH 7.51, PaCO₂ 47, bicarb 36. Pause and try it.
Alkalosis. Bicarb matches the pH, so it's metabolic. The lungs hold on to CO₂, but the pH is still abnormal: partially compensated metabolic alkalosis.
