🫁 NBRC practice: high flow or BiPAP
High flow or bilevel? Is the problem oxygen, or CO₂?
HFNC: heated, humidified, up to 60 L/min, about 1 cm H₂O of PEEP per 10 L/min with the mouth closed. BiPAP: the IPAP − EPAP gap is pressure support, and it moves CO₂.
