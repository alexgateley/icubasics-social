🫁 NBRC practice: PEEP and blood pressure
You turned up the PEEP and the blood pressure dropped. Why?
PEEP opens alveoli but squeezes venous return. Raise it 2–3 at a time. Trapped air (auto-PEEP)? Set the vent so there's time to breathe out fully.
