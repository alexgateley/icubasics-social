🫁 NBRC practice: ROME for ABGs
ROME: the four-letter trick for reading ABGs.
Respiratory Opposite: pH and PaCO₂ move in opposite directions. Metabolic Equal: pH and bicarb move the same way. Whichever matches the pH is the cause.
