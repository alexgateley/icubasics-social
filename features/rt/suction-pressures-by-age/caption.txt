🫁 NBRC practice: suction pressures by age
Suctioning isn't harmless. It can drop the saturation, cause bleeding, and cost lung volume.
AARC 2022: adults below 200 mm Hg, neonates and children below 120. Catheter blocks less than half the tube (under 70% in neonates). 15 seconds max.
