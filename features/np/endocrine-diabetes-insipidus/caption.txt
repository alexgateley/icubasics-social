🩺 FNP boards practice: central vs nephrogenic diabetes insipidus
Central DI: too little ADH (head injury, pituitary surgery, tumor). Nephrogenic DI: the kidneys ignore it (lithium, high calcium).
Huge volumes of very dilute urine and intense thirst. If they can't drink, the sodium climbs.
Central DI gets desmopressin; nephrogenic won't respond. Desmopressin's boxed warning is low sodium.
