🩺 FNP boards practice: why SSRIs take weeks
Usually 4–8 weeks. Sleep, appetite and energy often improve before mood does.
Under 25, suicidal thoughts can increase. Watch everyone closely, especially in the first few weeks.
Slow to work doesn't mean not working. Don't stop suddenly.
