🩺 FNP boards practice: lithium and low sodium
The kidney handles lithium much like sodium: run low on sodium and it clears less lithium, so the level climbs.
Fever, vomiting, diarrhea and heavy sweating can do it. So can thiazides, ACE inhibitors, ARBs and NSAIDs.
Therapeutic about 0.8–1.2; toxicity starts around 1.5. Early signs: coarse tremor, vomiting, diarrhea.
