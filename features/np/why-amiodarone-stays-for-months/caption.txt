🩺 FNP boards practice: why amiodarone stays for months
It soaks into fat, liver and lung. Its half-life averages weeks, not hours.
Long term it can injure the lungs, thyroid, liver and eyes. It also boosts warfarin and digoxin.
Stopping it doesn't end it: effects and interactions can last weeks to months after the last dose.
