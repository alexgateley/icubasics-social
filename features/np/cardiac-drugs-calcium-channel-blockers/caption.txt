🩺 FNP boards practice: calcium channel blockers, amlodipine vs diltiazem
Dipines (amlodipine, nifedipine) dilate arteries and don't slow the AV node. The ankle swelling (about 11% on amlodipine 10 mg) isn't fluid overload, so a diuretic isn't the fix.
Diltiazem and verapamil slow rate and AV conduction and weaken the squeeze: not with a systolic under 90, and not in reduced-EF heart failure.
Adding a beta blocker? Watch for heart block.
