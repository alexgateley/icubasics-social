🩺 FNP boards practice: DOAC reversal
Dabigatran blocks thrombin and has its own antidote: idarucizumab, 5 g IV.
Apixaban and rivaroxaban block factor Xa. Andexanet alfa left the US market in December 2025, so 4-factor PCC (off label) is the usual choice.
Reverse for life-threatening bleeding or emergency surgery, not just because they take one. Vitamin K and protamine won't reverse these drugs.
