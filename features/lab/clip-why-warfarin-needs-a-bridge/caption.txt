🧪 ASCP MLS/MLT practice: why warfarin needs a bridge
Protein C has a half-life of about 8 hours, so it falls early. Prothrombin (factor II) lasts about 60 hours, so the INR can rise before real protection arrives.
Heparin or enoxaparin continues for at least 5 days AND until the INR has been 2 or higher for at least 24 hours.
