🧪 ASCP MLS/MLT practice: refeeding syndrome
Insulin drives phosphate, potassium and magnesium into cells, and blood levels crash, usually within 2 to 5 days.
Low phosphate is the hallmark.
Electrolytes are checked and replaced daily at first.
