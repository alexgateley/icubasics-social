🧪 ASCP MLS/MLT practice: septic shock pressors
Norepinephrine is first line in septic shock.
If doses keep climbing, guidelines suggest adding vasopressin; epinephrine can be added next.
Norepinephrine leaking into tissue can cause necrosis; the label calls for phentolamine.
