🧪 ASCP MLS/MLT practice: why calcium falls in massive transfusion
Every bag holds citrate, which keeps stored blood from clotting by binding calcium. The liver normally clears it in minutes.
In shock the liver gets too little blood flow, citrate builds up and ionized calcium drops: weaker clotting, weaker heart.
Cold, acid and poor clotting are the lethal triad; add low calcium and it's the lethal diamond.
