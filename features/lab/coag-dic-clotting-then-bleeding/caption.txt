🧪 ASCP MLS/MLT practice: DIC, clotting then bleeding
A trigger like sepsis, trauma or placental abruption switches on clotting all through the body and uses up platelets, fibrinogen and clotting factors.
The labs match: platelets low, fibrinogen low, PT and PTT long, D-dimer high.
Treat the cause first; if bleeding, replace what's missing: platelets, cryo for fibrinogen, plasma for factors.
