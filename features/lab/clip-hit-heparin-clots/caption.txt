🧪 ASCP MLS/MLT practice: HIT (heparin-induced thrombocytopenia)
An immune reaction: platelets fall, usually 5–10 days after starting heparin, or sooner with heparin in the last month.
The danger is clotting, not bleeding.
Platelets are checked before heparin and regularly during it.
