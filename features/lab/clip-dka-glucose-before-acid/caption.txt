🧪 ASCP MLS/MLT practice: DKA: glucose falls before the acid clears
Glucose falls long before the acid clears. Below 250: dextrose is added and the insulin keeps running.
Glucose every 1 to 2 hours; electrolytes, venous pH and ketones about every 4.
Many protocols watch the anion gap close. The 2024 consensus goes by ketones plus pH or bicarb.
