🧪 ASCP MLS/MLT practice: insulin onset, peak and duration
Rapid (lispro, aspart): onset about 15 min, peak 1–2 h. Regular: onset 30–60 min, peak 2–4 h.
NPH: peak 4–12 h. Glargine: no pronounced peak, lasts about 24 h.
A low glucose is most likely near the peak. Times are approximate and sources differ.
