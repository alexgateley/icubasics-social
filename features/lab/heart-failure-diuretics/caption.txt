🧪 ASCP MLS/MLT practice: diuretics and electrolytes
Loop and thiazide diuretics: K⁺ ↓ and Mg²⁺ ↓ (thiazides can raise Ca²⁺).
Spironolactone: K⁺ ↑, so the label says check potassium within a week of starting it.
