🧪 ASCP MLS/MLT practice: O for red cells, AB for plasma
Red cells: the patient's antibodies meet the donor's markers. O carries no A or B marker, so O is the universal red cell donor.
Plasma flips it: the donor's antibodies meet the patient's cells. AB plasma has no anti-A and no anti-B, so it's the universal plasma donor.
Type unknown? O negative goes to girls and women who could become pregnant.
