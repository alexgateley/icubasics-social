🧪 ASCP MLS/MLT practice: ROME for ABGs
Respiratory Opposite: pH and PaCO2 move in opposite directions. Metabolic Equal: pH and HCO3 move together.
Whichever value matches the pH is the primary process.
Teaching ranges: pH 7.35–7.45, PaCO2 35–45, HCO3 22–26. Ranges vary by lab.
