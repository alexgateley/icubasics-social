🧪 ASCP MLS/MLT practice: aPTT vs INR
Warfarin gets the PT and INR. Heparin gets the extra T: the aPTT, or an anti-Xa level.
Heparin: aPTT often about 1.5–2.5× control, or anti-Xa often 0.3–0.7. Warfarin INR: often 2–3.
Enoxaparin: no routine lab; if a level is needed, it's anti-Xa. DOACs: no routine INR.
