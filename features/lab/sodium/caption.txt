🧪 ASCP MLS/MLT practice: sodium
Normal about 135–145 mEq/L; ranges vary by lab. Water follows sodium.
Low sodium: water shifts into brain cells and they swell. High sodium: water leaves and they shrink.
Raise chronic low sodium too fast and you risk osmotic demyelination. A common limit: about 10 mEq/L in 24 h.
