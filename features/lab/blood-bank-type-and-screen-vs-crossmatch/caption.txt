🧪 ASCP MLS/MLT practice: type and screen vs type and crossmatch
Type and screen: ABO/Rh plus an antibody screen of the plasma, for when blood might be needed but probably won't be.
Type and crossmatch adds a step: specific donor units are tested against this patient's plasma, for a high bleeding risk.
No time? Uncrossmatched group O red cells can be released; draw the blood bank sample before that first unit if you can.
