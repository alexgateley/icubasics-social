🧪 ASCP MLS/MLT practice: daily weights and BNP
A BNP of 100 or more supports heart failure in the hospital.
Daily weights: same scale, every morning, after voiding. More than 2–3 lb in a day or 5 lb in a week is the commonly taught rule.
