CCRN practice: a smooth, rounded A-line. Good trace, or a problem?
A problem. A good trace has a sharp upstroke and a dicrotic notch, and a fast flush shows a square with one or two bounces. Overdamped reads systolic falsely low and diastolic falsely high; underdamped overshoots; the MAP changes least.
