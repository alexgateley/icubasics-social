CCRN practice: which sedative lets your patient keep breathing on their own?
Dexmedetomidine: an alpha-2 agonist with little to no respiratory depression, so it does not have to be stopped before extubation. The price is the heart (bradycardia, hypotension). Heart rate in the 40s? Assess and follow your parameters; do not turn it up.
