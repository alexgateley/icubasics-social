Isotonic in the bag. Hypotonic in the patient 🧠
D5W starts around 250 mOsm/L, then the dextrose is metabolized and what's left is water. The label warns it can become extremely hypotonic and cause hyponatremia.
Watch it in brain injury, raised ICP, SIADH and after surgery.
