Liters of saline, and now a normal-gap acidosis? 🧪
Saline: 154 chloride, no buffer. LR: 109 plus lactate. Plasma-Lyte: 98 plus acetate and gluconate.
Strong ion difference kept simple: sodium minus chloride. About 40 in plasma, zero in saline. Give liters and the gap shrinks, and bicarbonate falls.
