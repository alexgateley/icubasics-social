🚑 CEN practice: HIT, the heparin complication that causes clots
HIT is an immune reaction: platelets fall, usually 5 to 10 days after starting heparin.
Platelets are checked before and during heparin.
Suspected HIT: stop all heparin and start a non-heparin anticoagulant, such as argatroban or bivalirudin.
