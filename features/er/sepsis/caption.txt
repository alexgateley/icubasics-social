🚑 CEN practice: sepsis, the first hour
Think sepsis: infection + new confusion, fast breathing, or low blood pressure. Don't rely on qSOFA alone.
First hour: lactate, blood cultures before antibiotics (don't delay them), broad-spectrum antibiotics ideally within 1 h when shock or sepsis is likely.
30 mL/kg crystalloid for low BP or lactate ≥ 4; norepinephrine first if MAP stays < 65.
