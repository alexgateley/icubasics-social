🚑 CEN practice: DKA with a normal glucose
On an SGLT2 inhibitor (-gliflozin), the glucose can stay fairly low while ketones build up.
Nausea, vomiting, belly pain? Check ketones and a blood gas, even if the glucose looks fine.
