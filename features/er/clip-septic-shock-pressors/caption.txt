🚑 CEN practice: septic shock pressors before a central line
Guidelines suggest starting vasopressors peripherally rather than delaying. Norepinephrine is first line.
Doses climbing? Guidelines suggest adding vasopressin; epinephrine can be added next.
Watch the IV site: norepinephrine leaking into tissue can cause necrosis; the label calls for phentolamine.
