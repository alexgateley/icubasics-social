🚑 CEN practice: magnesium toxicity
Reflexes usually fade first (around 10 mg/dL); breathing slows from about 12. Common hold: no patellar reflex, RR under 12, urine under about 30 mL/h.
Toxicity: stop the infusion, support breathing, give calcium gluconate.
