🚑 CEN practice: sepsis vs septic shock
Sepsis: life-threatening organ dysfunction caused by a dysregulated host response to infection.
Septic shock: a vasopressor needed to keep the MAP at 65 or higher, and lactate still above 2, despite adequate fluids.
Hospital mortality in septic shock was over 40 percent in the Sepsis-3 data.
