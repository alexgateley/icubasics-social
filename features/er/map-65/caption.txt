🚑 CEN practice: MAP 65 in septic shock
MAP = mean arterial pressure, the pressure driving blood into organs. Estimate it: DBP + ⅓ pulse pressure, so 90/50 ≈ 63.
Surviving Sepsis: initial MAP target 65 in septic shock on vasopressors. Aiming for 80–85 (SEPSISPAM) didn't lower deaths.
65 is a start: also check lactate, urine output, mentation and cap refill.
