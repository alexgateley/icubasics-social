🚑 CEN practice: which diabetes pills cause lows
The insulin pushers: sulfonylureas (glipizide, glyburide, glimepiride) and meglitinides. Metformin alone rarely does.
Sulfonylurea lows can last a day or two: keep rechecking after the number comes up. These patients often need admission.
