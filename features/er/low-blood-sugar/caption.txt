🚑 CEN practice: glucose 62, now what?
Under 70 mg/dL is low. Alert and able to swallow? Rule of 15: 15 grams of fast carbs, recheck in 15 minutes.
Not alert or can't swallow: nothing by mouth. Glucagon or IV dextrose per protocol.
Beta-blockers can hide the fast heartbeat; sweating may still show.
