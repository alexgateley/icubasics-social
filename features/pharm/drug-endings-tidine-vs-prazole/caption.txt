💊 PTCE practice: -tidine vs -prazole
Famotidine (-tidine) is an H2 blocker. Pantoprazole and omeprazole (-prazole) are proton pump inhibitors.
Omeprazole: usually once a day, before a meal, not chewed or crushed.
Trap: aripiprazole ends in -prazole, but it's an antipsychotic.
