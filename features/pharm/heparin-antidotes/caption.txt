💊 PTCE practice: anticoagulant antidotes
Heparin gets protamine: about 1 mg per 100 units, no more than 50 mg over 10 minutes.
Warfarin gets vitamin K, plus 4-factor PCC for major bleeding.
Don't cross them: vitamin K won't reverse heparin, and protamine won't reverse warfarin.
