💊 PTCE practice: lithium interactions
Thiazides, ACE inhibitors, ARBs and NSAIDs can raise the lithium level.
So can fever, vomiting, diarrhea and heavy sweating: the kidney handles lithium much like sodium.
Therapeutic about 0.8–1.2; toxicity starts around 1.5.
