💊 PTCE practice: pounds to kilograms
kg = lb ÷ 2.2.
Fentanyl 1 mcg/kg at 154 lb: 70 kg, 70 mcg, 1.4 mL of 50 mcg/mL.
Skip the conversion and the dose is 2.2 times too big.
