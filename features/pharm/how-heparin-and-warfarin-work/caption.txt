💊 PTCE practice: heparin vs warfarin
Heparin supercharges antithrombin. Warfarin stops the liver building new clotting factors, so its full effect takes days.
Tests: aPTT (heparin), INR (warfarin). Antidotes: protamine, vitamin K.
Neither one dissolves a clot already there. They stop it growing.
