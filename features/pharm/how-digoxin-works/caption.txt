💊 PTCE practice: digoxin
Digoxin jams the sodium-potassium pump: a stronger squeeze and a slower AV node.
Narrow window, cleared by the kidneys. Low potassium or low magnesium makes it more toxic.
Toxicity: nausea, vomiting, vision changes, confusion, new arrhythmias.
