💊 PTCE practice: amiodarone
Its half-life averages weeks: it soaks into fat, liver and lung.
It boosts warfarin and digoxin, and effects and interactions can last weeks to months after the last dose.
Long term it can injure the lungs, thyroid, liver and eyes.
