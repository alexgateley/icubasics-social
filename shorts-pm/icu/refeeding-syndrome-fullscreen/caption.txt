Tube feeds started, and two days later the phosphate is on the floor 📉
Refeeding syndrome: insulin drives phosphate, potassium and magnesium into cells, usually within 2 to 5 days of feeding.
Low phosphate is the hallmark, with heart failure, arrhythmias and respiratory failure behind it.
High risk: BMI under 16, little or no intake for over 10 days, low levels before feeding.
Thiamine before feeding, start low, go slow, replace electrolytes daily at first.
