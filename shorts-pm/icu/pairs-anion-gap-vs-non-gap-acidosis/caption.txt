Two patients, same low pH, same low bicarb. One has DKA, one has diarrhea. One subtraction tells them apart.
High gap: acid added (ketones, lactate, renal failure, toxins). Normal gap: usually bicarb lost, chloride up. Low albumin hides a high gap: Merck lowers the normal gap about 2.5 for every 1 g/dL fall in albumin.
