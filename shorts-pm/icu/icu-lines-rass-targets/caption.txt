CCRN practice: RASS minus 2. Too deep, or on target?
On target for most ventilated adults: light sedation, about −2 to +1. Score in order (watch, voice, touch), go deeper only for a reason like a paralytic, and pair a daily wake-up trial with a breathing trial.
