🫀 ED/ICU pharm: chronic lithium toxicity
Dehydration or a new NSAID, ACE inhibitor or thiazide can bring serious neuro signs at levels as low as 1.5 mEq/L. Charcoal doesn't bind lithium; severe cases go to dialysis.
