CCRN practice: the bed went up and the A-line read higher. What changed?
The transducer level, not the patient. Level to the phlebostatic axis (4th intercostal space, mid-axillary line: the right atrium). Below reads falsely high, above falsely low, about 7.5 mm Hg per 10 cm, so re-level whenever the bed or patient moves.
