CCRN practice: propofol started and the BP fell. Bad luck, or the drug?
The drug. Propofol relaxes the arteries and lowers preload, and the label says BP can fall more than 30% at induction. Correct the fluid deficit first, skip rapid boluses in fragile patients, and treat pain with an analgesic, not more propofol.
