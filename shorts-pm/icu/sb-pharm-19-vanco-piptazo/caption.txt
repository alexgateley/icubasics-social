Vancomycin plus pip tazo: why does the creatinine jump?
In one ICU cohort, creatinine rose but cystatin C, dialysis and death showed no clear difference. Labels still warn of more AKI with the pair. Vancomycin plus an aminoglycoside is a true kidney pair: the label says avoid it.
