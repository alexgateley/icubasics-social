CCRN practice: CVP of 10. Does the patient need fluid?
That number cannot tell you. PEEP, right heart failure, tricuspid disease, a pericardial effusion and abdominal pressure all push it up without adding volume. Extremes and a rising CVP still matter; to ask if fluid will help, use a dynamic test like a passive leg raise with cardiac output.
