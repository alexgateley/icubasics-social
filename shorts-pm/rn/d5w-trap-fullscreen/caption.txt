D5W looks isotonic in the bag. Your patient's cells disagree 💧
In the bag it's about 250 mOsm/L, and the label calls it isotonic. But the body burns the dextrose quickly and leaves water, so it acts hypotonic.
Avoid hypotonic fluids in brain injury or raised ICP, and in patients prone to low sodium: children, SIADH, after surgery.
