A malnourished patient starts eating, and days later the labs crash. Why? 🍽️
Refeeding syndrome: insulin drives phosphate, potassium and magnesium into cells, usually within 2 to 5 days.
Low phosphate is the hallmark. Heart failure, arrhythmias and respiratory failure can follow.
Useful on transports and for anyone who wants the why behind the labs.
