Climb in an aircraft, and every pocket of trapped gas in your patient gets bigger.
Pressure down, volume up: at an 8,000 ft cabin trapped gas grows by about a third. The ETT cuff, a pneumothorax and gut gas all grow on the climb, so check the cuff pressure with a manometer on the climb and again on the descent.
