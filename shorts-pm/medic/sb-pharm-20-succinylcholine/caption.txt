Succinylcholine is the paralytic that raises potassium.
Ask about potassium risk before you push. If it's there, a non-depolarizing paralytic like rocuronium is a common alternative, per protocol. Watch for malignant hyperthermia: jaw spasm, rising CO2, tachycardia, heat.
