🫀 CCRN safety: epinephrine 1 mg/mL vs 0.1 mg/mL
Arrest: 1 mg = 10 mL of 0.1 mg/mL. Anaphylaxis IM: 0.3–0.5 mL of 1 mg/mL. Say the concentration out loud.
